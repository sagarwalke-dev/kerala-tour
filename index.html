Day 1

Dec 24 (Thu) • Hyderabad → Kochi → Munnar

📍 Stay: Munnar (Night 1)

Board early morning flight from Hyderabad (recommended 06:00 AM SpiceJet SG 703 or 10:55 AM IndiGo) to Cochin International Airport (COK). Meet your cab driver and start the scenic mountain ascent via NH 85.

  • • En Route: Stop at Cheeyappara and Valara stepped waterfalls for roadside photography and fresh coconut water.
  • • 5:00 PM Arrival: Check into your Munnar resort in daylight. Evenings get foggy and chilly quickly; enjoy hot cardamom tea from your balcony overlooking the tea valleys.
Day 1 Logistics
Airport → Munnar:107 km (~3.5 hrs)
Target Arrival:~5:00 PM
Work Status:Holiday declared
Day 2

Dec 25 (Fri) • Christmas in the Munnar Hills

📍 Stay: Munnar (Night 2) • Leave 1/5

Full day exploring Munnar’s iconic attractions. Start early to avoid holiday queues at the national park gates.

Morning (08:30 AM): Eravikulam National Park (Rajamalai). Take the forest department bus to spot the endangered Nilgiri Tahr mountain goat (₹200 ticket).
Midday: KDHP Tata Tea Museum (₹150 ticket) to watch tea processing from white to black tea and sample fresh tea leaves.
Afternoon: Mattupetty Dam reservoir & Echo Point shouting valley surrounded by Western Ghats ridges.
Late Afternoon: Munnar Rose Garden / Floriculture Centre (₹50 entry) with flower terrace viewpoints.
Key Tips
Pre-book Eravikulam tickets online to skip 1-hour ticket queues. Carry light woolens as temperatures drop to 12°C in Munnar at night.
Day 3

Dec 26 (Sat) • Munnar → Thekkady (Kumily)

📍 Stay: Thekkady (Night 3)

Check out at 09:00 AM and take the scenic Lockhart Gap Road toward Thekkady. The road winds through dense cardamom and pepper forests.

  • • En route Adventure: Optional longest river-crossing flying fox zip-line near Anachal (~₹1,400 with GoPro footage).
  • • Spice Plantation Tour: 1-hour guided walking tour explaining medicinal herbs (shatavari, bhringraj, vanilla, cinnamon). Buy authentic unadulterated spices directly from estate outlets.
  • • Evening Cultural Shows (5:30 PM & 7:00 PM): Live theatrical Kathakali classical dance and high-octane Kalaripayattu martial arts (~₹300 combo).
Transit Info
Distance:90 km (~3 hrs)
Route:Munnar-Kumily Hwy
Activity Cost:Spice: ₹100 | Show: ₹300
Day 4

Dec 27 (Sun) • Thekkady → Alleppey (Alappuzha)

📍 Stay: Alleppey (Night 4)

Early morning wildlife sighting inside Periyar Tiger Reserve, followed by a downhill road drive to the Arabian backwaters.

  • • 07:30 AM Periyar Lake Boat Safari: 1.5-hour boating on the reservoir to view wild gaur (bison), sambar deer, and birds along the water’s edge (Bus: ₹75, Boat: ₹255).
  • • Drive to Backwaters: Descend via Kottayam toward Alleppey (~4 hours). Check into your private houseboat or waterfront lagoon resort.
  • • Sunset: Watch the sunset over Vembanad Lake or visit Alleppey Beach and its historic 160-year-old pier.
Transit Info
Distance:139 km (~4 hrs)
Route:NH 183 via Kottayam
Day 5

Dec 28 (Mon) • Alleppey Canal Waterways & Kuttanad Life

📍 Stay: Alleppey (Night 5) • Leave 2/5

Dedicate the day to authentic village life away from big tourist boats. Houseboats stay in wide channels, while small motorized Shikaras and hand-rowed canoes navigate the interior canals where locals live.

  • • Morning Shikara / Canoe Ride (~₹1,000 for 2 hrs): Drift under canopy bridges, watching ducks, village schools, and coconut lagoon paths.
  • • Authentic Kuttanad Lunch: Enjoy hot parottas, red rice, pearl spot fish (Karimeen Pollichathu), or Kerala Sadhya served on banana leaf.
  • • Evening: Unwind with fresh tender coconut water and peaceful backwater sunsets.
Pacing Highlight
Having 2 nights in Alleppey eliminates packing and checking out for a day, giving you a relaxing pause right before the coastal stretch.
Day 6

Dec 29 (Tue) • Alleppey → Jatayu Earth’s Center → Varkala

📍 Stay: Varkala (Night 6) • Leave 3/5

Travel south toward the Arabian coastline with a stop at Chadayamangalam to visit the world's largest bird sculpture.

  • • Jatayu Earth’s Center (11:30 AM – 2:00 PM): Take the Swiss cable car up the rocky hill (entry ~₹600 with cable car) to witness the 200-ft giant Jatayu sculpture and panoramic landscape views.
  • • Reach Varkala (3:30 PM): Check into your cliff-top resort.
  • • North Cliff Sunset: Walk along the cliff path overlooking Papanasam Beach; sip iced coffee at Bohemian cliff cafes as the sun sinks into the Arabian Sea.
Transit Info
Alleppey → Jatayu:102 km (~2.5 hrs)
Jatayu → Varkala:37 km (~1 hr)
Day 7

Dec 30 (Wed) • Varkala Beaches, Water Sports & Cliff Cafes

📍 Stay: Varkala (Night 7) • Leave 4/5

Full relaxing day along Kerala's premier cliff beach. Experience coastal adventures or lounge by the sea.

  • • Morning Coastal Paramotoring (Optional): Fly over the red cliffs and the coastline with Fly Varkala (~₹4,500 + ₹500 video).
  • • Beach Explorations: Explore South Cliff, Odayam Beach, and Black Sand Beach.
  • • Evening Vibe: Browse Tibetan handicraft stores, aromatic spice stalls, and try fresh seafood or woodfired pizza at cliffside cafes.
Highlights
Varkala offers a relaxed hippie coastal vibe similar to Goa's cliffs, but with unique reddish laterite formations.
Day 8

Dec 31 (Thu) • Poovar Mangrove Estuary & Check-in Near Temple

📍 Stay: Trivandrum (Night 8) • Leave 5/5

Check out from Varkala around 09:30 AM and drive south past Trivandrum down to Poovar Island.

  • • Poovar Island Boat Safari (12:30 PM – 2:00 PM): 1-hour cruise through dense mangrove waterways out to Golden Sand Beach, where the Neyyar River meets the Arabian Sea (~₹2,000/boat).
  • • Check-in Near Temple (5:00 PM): Check in at a hotel in East Fort / Pazhavangadi, walking distance from Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple.
  • • New Year’s Eve Prep: Buy/iron your Dhoti/Mundu and Saree for tomorrow morning's darshan. Enjoy dinner and rest early to beat the morning rush.
Key Strategic Move
Staying near East Fort tonight ensures you can simply walk to the temple at 05:45 AM without cab delays, morning traffic, or parking hassles.
Day 9

Jan 1 (Fri) • New Year Darshan & Azhimala Shiva Temple

📍 Stay: Trivandrum (Night 9) • Holiday

Start the first day of the year with sacred morning blessings at the world-renowned Padmanabhaswamy Temple, followed by coastal heritage sights.

  • • 05:45 AM – 07:30 AM: Walk to the East Gate for the 06:30 AM Darshan slot. View the majestic 18-foot reclining Ananthasayanam idol across three temple doors.
  • • Afternoon: Drive 20 mins to Azhimala Shiva Temple to see the dramatic 58-foot seaside Gangadhareswara Shiva statue carved against ocean waves.
  • • Sunset at Kovalam: Climb the 120-foot Kovalam Lighthouse (₹20 entry) for 360-degree aerial views of Lighthouse and Hawa beaches.
Temple Dress Code
• Men: Plain Dhoti/Mundu (bare torso; shoulder shawl allowed).
• Women: Saree or Set-Mundu only.
• Prohibited: Phones, watches, pants, leather.

Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple • New Year Darshan Guide

Crucial rules & preparation for January 1st early morning visit

⏰ Recommended Darshan Slots

Target Slot (Recommended): 06:30 AM – 07:00 AM. Line up outside East Gate by 05:45 AM. It offers natural morning light and a manageable queue.
Nirmalya Darshanam: 03:30 AM – 04:45 AM (Strictly for very early risers; holy first viewing).
Mid-Morning Slot: 08:30 AM – 10:00 AM (Queue can stretch up to 2 hours on Jan 1).

👘 Strict Kerala Dress Code

Men: Must wear a Dhoti / Mundu around the waist. Chest/torso must remain bare. An Angavastram (shawl) may be draped over the shoulders. Pants, shirts, and dhotis wrapped over trousers are strictly turned away.
Women: Must wear a traditional Saree or Set-Mundu. Young girls can wear long skirts/pavada. Salwar-kameez, jeans, and western apparel are strictly barred.
💡 Dhotis are readily available in shops outside the temple gates for ~₹100–₹150 if needed.

🚫 Electronic & Belongings Ban

Due to high-security classification and hidden vault treasures, the temple maintains zero tolerance for:

  • Mobile phones & smartwatches
  • Cameras or digital electronics
  • Leather belts, pouches, and big wallets
Why East Fort Stay is Ideal: Since your hotel is just a 5-minute walk from East Fort, leave all gadgets safely locked inside your hotel room safe and carry only cash/ID!
