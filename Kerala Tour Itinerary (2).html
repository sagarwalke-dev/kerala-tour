📐 Why Operators Add 250 km & The Real Math

In Kerala, hiring a cab from Kochi Airport and dismissing it at Trivandrum is a linear one-way trip. Standard contracts calculate mileage "Garage-to-Garage", meaning the vehicle must return to its home garage in Nedumbassery (Kochi).

Your Actual Sightseeing Running:~880 km
Return Empty Run (TVC → Kochi):+240 km
Total Garage-to-Garage Running:~1,120 km
The Financial Reality: Standard packages include 1,000 km (100 km/day × 10 days). A 1,120 km trip creates only ~120 km of overage. At ₹15/km for an Ertiga, that equals only ~₹1,650 to ₹1,800 extra—not ₹5,000 or ₹8,000!

💡 Sagar's Negotiation Script

When operators mention the 250 km return, ask for this clean package:

"Our actual running is ~880 km. With 240 km return empty, garage-to-garage is ~1,120 km. Please quote a fixed 1,200 km all-inclusive 10-day slab for an Ertiga including driver bata, tolls, and return run."

🚿 Driver Washroom & Refreshment Norms in Kerala

What is Driver Bata? You pay ₹400–₹500/day bundled in your quote. This allowance is meant for the driver's own meals and commercial dormitory bed. You are not obligated to book a room for him.
Morning Washroom Access Almost all budget hotels and homestays allow the driver to use the common lobby or staff washroom to shower and brush before morning pickup.
Where do Drivers Sleep? Properties in Munnar & Thekkady offer driver dorms (~₹200). In Alleppey, Varkala, and Kovalam, drivers stay in dedicated tourist driver lodges near the beach parking areas.

Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple • New Year Darshan Guide

Crucial rules & preparation for January 1st early morning visit (Driving from Kovalam)

⏰ Recommended Darshan Slots

Target Slot (Recommended): 06:30 AM – 07:00 AM. Leave Kovalam in your cab by 05:15 AM to line up outside East Gate by 05:45 AM.
Nirmalya Darshanam: 03:30 AM – 04:45 AM (Strictly for very early risers; holy first viewing).
Mid-Morning Slot: 08:30 AM – 10:00 AM (Queue can stretch over 2 hours on Jan 1).

👘 Strict Kerala Dress Code

Men: Must wear a Dhoti / Mundu around the waist. Chest/torso must remain bare. An Angavastram (shawl) may be draped over shoulders. Pants, shirts, or dhotis wrapped over trousers are strictly turned away.
Women: Must wear a traditional Saree or Set-Mundu. Young girls can wear long skirts/pavada. Salwar-kameez, jeans, and western apparel are strictly barred.
💡 Dhotis are readily available in shops outside the East Gate for ~₹100–₹150 if needed.

🚫 Electronic & Belongings Ban

Due to high-security classification and hidden vault treasures, the temple maintains zero tolerance for:

  • Mobile phones & smartwatches
  • Cameras or digital electronics
  • Leather belts, pouches, and big wallets
Cab Storage Advantage: Because you are traveling via your dedicated cab from Kovalam, you can leave all phones, watches, and leather items locked securely in the car with your driver!
